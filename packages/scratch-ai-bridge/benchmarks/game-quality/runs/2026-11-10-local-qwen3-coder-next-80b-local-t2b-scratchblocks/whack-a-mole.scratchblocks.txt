set [score v] to (0)  
set [time v] to (30)  
hide  
forever  
  wait (random (1) to (3)) seconds  
  go to x: (random (-150) to (150)) y: (random (-100) to (100))  
  show  
  wait (1) seconds  
  hide  
end  

when this sprite clicked  
change [score v] by (1)  
play sound [Pop v] until done  
hide  
wait (0.5) seconds  
show  

when [space v] key pressed  
broadcast [start v]  

when [start v] received  
set [time v] to (30)  
show  
repeat (30)  
  wait (1) seconds  
  change [time v] by (-1)  
end  
hide  
say [Game Over!] for (2) seconds  
show
